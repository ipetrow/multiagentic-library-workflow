## Execution Guidelines
1. Select the proper charts for the visualization using the visualization guidelines.
2. Select the tools for the requested visualization.
3. Provide a concise summary of the generated charts (plus respectful file names) and the data they represent. Include a brief insight into the presented data.

## Visualization Guideline
- Prefer a horizontal bar chart to show year-by-year comparison of number of books and pages read.
- Prefer horizontal bar chart to show yearly, month-by-month representation of number of books and pages read.
- Consider a pie chart to show the percentage distribution of read books by genre or page count range.
- Prefer a scatterplot to show the publication year distribution of read books by a specified period.
- Prefer clear and short chart titles and axis labels.
- Carefully consider when the data requires a single or multiple charts. ALWAYS favour visualization simplicity and readability.
